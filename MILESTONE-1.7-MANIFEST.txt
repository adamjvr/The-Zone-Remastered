Promoted behavior:
  - EXPL retains previous_type and uses recovered 0x12080 cadence.
  - ship/moth/base explosions advance every other Classic action pass.
  - ordinary recovered explosion origins advance every action pass.
  - player reset is driven by ship-explosion completion, not 120 ticks.
  - respawn center is recovered 320,240 in ZoneCore center coordinates.
  - Mother/HQ objective count decrements when its EXPL finalizes.
  - last Mother/HQ finalization immediately triggers next fixed wave.
  - surviving defenders do not gate wave completion.
  - provisional 90-tick wave-clear countdown is removed.
  - projectile 90/120 retirement counters are explicitly documented as temporary;
    recovered SHOT/FIRE actions have no countdown and await +128 spatial/list parity.

Unchanged intentionally:
  - 720-Hz continuous dynamics architecture
  - Classic AI/RNG/fire/timer decision cadence
  - Classic-boundary collision sampling
  - AVAudioEngine backend
  - Metal presentation
  - projectile retirement behavior (pending spatial/list lift)
  - procedural Waves 19+
